# Photo Assets for the Six Websites — Instructions

`PHOTO_ASSETS_MANIFEST.json` contains direct image URLs from the 2GIS gallery filter labelled **“Owner-uploaded”**. User-uploaded photos were excluded.

## Important
- These are source URLs, not image files already downloaded into the project.
- The 2GIS owner-uploaded label identifies who uploaded an image; it is not a separate licence to republish the image on an external commercial website. Obtain permission from the relevant business or copyright holder before publication.
- Do not replace missing photos with stock photography or AI-generated images.
- The image binaries are not stored in this archive. Claude Code may use the URLs in the JSON manifest as download sources if network access is available, but must not bypass 2GIS access restrictions or licensing controls.
